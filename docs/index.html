Our mission

Every birth is unique. Your preparation should be too.

Recognizing the uniqueness of each birth and postpartum journey, I intentionally keep classes small to create a comfortable, individualized learning environment. It lets me focus on what you want to learn, give hands-on support, and make sure every question gets answered.

Alongside personalized education, I focus on empowering support persons — giving them the insight and practical skills to be an invaluable source of support in every part of this journey.

— Ashley, RN

What you’ll learn

Knowledge that turns worry into confidence

  • When to go to the hospital

    Recognize the signs of labour and know when it’s time to head in.

  • Your pain management options

    Understand both unmedicated and hospital-based options, so you can make informed choices.

  • Hands-on comfort techniques

    Massage, counter-pressure and positions drawn from midwives, yoga teachers and massage therapists.

  • The support person’s role

    Practical ways your partner or support person can help, every step of the way.

  • Your first 24 hours

    What to expect right after birth as you become a new family.

  • Newborn care, feeding & soothing

    In our full-day program: postpartum recovery, breastfeeding with a certified lactation consultant, bottle-feeding, and calming a crying baby.
