Good to know before you book

  • Who comes: you and one support person — a partner, family member, friend or doula.
  • When: ideally between 25 and 35 weeks, but you’re welcome at any stage.
  • Where: Ottawa Medical Group, 4289 Innes Road, 2nd floor (OMG obstetricians’ offices), Orléans.
  • Insurance: the full-day program is covered by most insurance companies — check your plan.
