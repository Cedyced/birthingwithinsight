FAQ

Your questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering before your class. Still curious? We’re always happy to chat.

Where are the classes held?

Our sessions are held in the Ottawa Medical Group building at 4289 Innes Road, Orléans, ON K1E 0A8, in the offices of the OMG obstetricians on the 2nd floor.

We look forward to welcoming you to our beautiful, warm space.

Who is welcome?

The registration fee includes the mother-to-be and one support person — whoever will be by your side, whether a partner, family member, friend or doula.

What can I expect?

Expect a 2.5-hour session where we guide both you and your support person through the entire labour and birthing process. This includes answering common pre-birth questions and preparing you for your first 24 hours as a new family.

We’ll explore both non-pharmacological and hospital-based pain management options. Our experienced nurses also teach hands-on techniques, drawing on insight from massage therapists, yoga teachers and midwives, for a comprehensive and practical learning experience.

When should I plan my session?

Any time between 25 and 35 weeks of pregnancy is ideal — however, everyone is welcome at any point in their pregnancy.

Are classes available in French?

Yes! We offer our in-person prenatal class in both English and French. See all classes.

Is the class covered by insurance?

Our Full Day Prenatal & Newborn Care program is covered by most insurance companies. We recommend checking with your provider.

Have more questions?

Want to find out more? Please don’t hesitate to contact us.
