About us

We’re so glad you found your way here.

I’m Ashley, founder of Birthing With Insight, and it fills me with joy to welcome you.

  • Registered NurseLabour & delivery since 2010
  • Montfort HospitalProfessional home for over a decade
  • Mother of twoI know the beauty and the challenges first-hand
  • Holistic trainingYoga, massage, midwifery & obstetrics

As a Registered Nurse working in labour and delivery since 2010, and a mother of two myself, I intimately understand both the beauty and the challenges of childbirth. I’ve had the privilege of serving expectant families in renowned hospitals across our region — and Montfort Hospital has been my professional home for over a decade, where I’ve nurtured my passion for supporting mothers and their partners through one of life’s most transformative experiences.

Along the way, I’ve integrated teachings from yoga teachers, massage therapists, midwives and obstetricians. This enriches my practice and allows me to offer truly hands-on support. It also means I can tailor care and teaching to each person’s unique needs, fostering a deeper sense of insight and well-being throughout birth and the postpartum period.

It’s my passion and privilege to support expectant families in person on their journey to parenthood, offering personalized birth preparation sessions, postpartum wellness support and newborn care. Whether you’re preparing for your upcoming birth, looking for support in the postpartum period, or are new to our healthcare system and want to understand what to expect — I’m here to give you the resources and support you need to move through this experience with confidence and joy.

I invite you to explore further and discover how we can take this journey together.
